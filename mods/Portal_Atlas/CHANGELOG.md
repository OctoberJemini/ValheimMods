# Changelog

## 0.2.12

- Add a centered native Dvergr orb and frame, blue sparks, localized light and quiet spatial hum to the Atlas pillar.
- Soften and lightly weather stone edges, including lower supports; correct cap geometry.
- Keep existing carvings, the front arrival marker, dimensions, costs and travel behavior. Existing pillars update when loaded.
- Confirmed loading and visually reviewed in game. Dedicated-server and broader multiplayer validation remain incomplete.

## 0.2.10

- Only the front base triangle glows orange, identifying the side where Atlas travellers arrive. The other three base triangles remain as dark stone insets.
- The direction marker appears on placed pillars and the build preview. Upper orange windows and the crown wisp retain their existing appearance.

## 0.2.9

- Refocused the store description and usage guide on Portal Atlas Map.
- Added Auto Mapping as an optional companion for automatically marking portals and discovered locations.
- Documentation-only update. The plugin DLL and approved icon are unchanged from 0.2.8.

## 0.2.8

First public release of Portal Atlas Map.

- Haldor sells the inventory/hotbar Atlas for 5,000 coins.
- Build wisp-crowned Atlas pillars for 20 Stone, 1 Wisp and 1 Surtling Core, without a workbench.
- Travel to normal portals and pillars throughout the world; normal portals open the destination picker without pairing.
- Search, favourites, renaming, native-style markers and a fast draggable destination list.
- Keeps ordinary-map controls separate from Atlas travel; adds passive nearby/discovered pillar markers.
- Includes corrected favourites across world reloads, Enter-to-rename and post-rename refresh.
- Approved map-and-pillar store icon with the shared OCT branding.
- Includes a full usage guide, backup instructions and direct bug-report links.
- 185 automated checks pass. Broader multiplayer/dedicated-server verification remains limited.
