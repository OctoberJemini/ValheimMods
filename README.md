## Reporting problems

Search existing issues, then choose the bug-report form. Select the affected mod and include its version, Valheim version, whether you play locally or on a server, reproduction steps, and a relevant log excerpt. Screenshots help with building and map issues. Do not upload world saves or credentials to a public issue.

This is a community mod project, not affiliated with Iron Gate. Downloads and dependency lists are on Thunderstore. Source snapshots here are not a guarantee of compatibility with every game update or mod combination.
